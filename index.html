PATIENT CONCIERGE GUIDE

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about preparing for your visit, insurance networks, same-day lab answers, and pavilion access.

Unlike standard clinics with 10-minute rushed consultations, Aura Clinic provides dedicated 45 to 60-minute initial visits in our sound-insulated private suites. Your physician will examine your comprehensive medical history, lifestyle factors, hereditary markers, and conduct non-hurried diagnostic reviews. You will leave with a clear, personalized health blueprint and direct follow-up channel.

We are in-network with premier private healthcare plans including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna Open Access, United Healthcare Choice Plus, and Medicare Part B. Our billing concierge coordinates pre-authorization on your behalf. We also provide transparent self-pay packages and detailed superbills for out-of-network reimbursement.

Because our automated hematology, biochemistry, and hormone pathology laboratory is located directly on campus, over 90% of routine diagnostic panels are processed within 90 minutes. Digital 3T MRI, ultrasounds, and echocardiograms are reviewed by board-certified radiologists on the same day and instantly synchronized to your private Patient Portal.

Yes. Every registered Aura patient receives access to our secure digital messaging portal. You can communicate directly with your physician’s care team for questions regarding treatment plans, medication adjustments, lab clarifications, or non-urgent symptoms 24 hours a day.

Yes. For routine follow-ups, medication reviews, lab interpretations, and dermatology screenings, we offer high-definition HIPAA-compliant telehealth consultations. You can select "Virtual Consultation" during appointment booking or request a tele-visit through your patient concierge.

Yes. Complimentary white-glove valet parking is located at our main entrance canopy (742 Evergreen Medical Pavilion). The facility is 100% ADA compliant with barrier-free elevator access, wide corridors, and private companion waiting rooms.

We understand schedules change. You may reschedule or cancel your visit with zero fee up to 24 hours prior to your consultation time directly via your confirmation link or SMS concierge. For cancellations under 24 hours, our desk will happily assist you in transferring your slot to a telehealth session.

Our in-house clinical pharmacy coordinates direct refills through the Patient Portal. We offer complimentary same-day courier delivery within metropolitan areas for urgent therapies, and coordinated mail orders for routine maintenance regimens.
